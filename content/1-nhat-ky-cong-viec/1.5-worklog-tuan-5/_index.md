## Week 5 Objectives:

* Study Elastic Load Balancer (ELB) and Auto Scaling services.
* Practice creating an Application Load Balancer to route traffic to EC2 instances.
* Configure an Auto Scaling Group to automatically increase/decrease the number of EC2 instances.
* Perform simulated load testing to check scalability and load balancing capabilities.
